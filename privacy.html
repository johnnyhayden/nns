Privacy Policy

NNS Chart Maker · Last updated October 30, 2026

NNS Chart Maker (nns-five.vercel.app) is a free tool for writing Nashville Number System charts. This page explains what information it uses and why.

Signing in with Google

If you choose to sign in, Google shares your account ID, email address, name and profile picture with the app. These are used only to identify you, keep your charts separate from other people's, and show who is signed in. The app does not request access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

Your charts

When you are signed in, the charts you save (title, key, tempo, time signature, songwriter, charted-by and chart text) are stored in a database hosted on Amazon Web Services, linked to your Google account ID. Only you can see them while signed in. If you are not signed in, charts are stored only in your own browser or in a folder on your computer that you choose.

Cookies

Signing in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 180 days. The app has no advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other sites.

Sharing

Your information is not sold or shared with anyone. It is processed only by the services that run the app: Google (sign-in), Vercel (web hosting) and Amazon Web Services (chart storage).

Deleting your data

You can delete any chart from the Saved Charts list at any time. To have all your charts and your account record removed, email johnnyhayden@gmail.com. You can also remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: johnnyhayden@gmail.com.
